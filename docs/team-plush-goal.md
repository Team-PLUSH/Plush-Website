## Founders' Mission

Ambitious missions always begin with a simple idea.

For Team Plush, it began with three high school students who believed that STEM could
be more than just innovation, but rather a secondary home where young engineers feel
welcomed, supported, and inspired one day at a time.

Team Plush is a newly established FIRST Robotics Competition (FRC) team based out of
Ottawa, Ontario. We want to share the equal level of opportunity to youth students
across the Ottawa community, where participation is hindered in comparison to
established regions like Toronto, California, or New York.

As a first-year team, we invite you to help us write the first chapter of Plush
Robotics! In return, we will provide you with exclusive updates on our build, credit
your support on social media, and add your logo on our robot. We need bold pioneering
partners like you who believe in hands-on STEM education.

## What's Team Plush?

Team Plush is a community FIRST Robotics Competition (FRC) team, assembled from a group
of passionate high school students building a functioning robot in under 3 months.

Philosophy to empower students to design, build, and test physical hardware without
judgement and with mentorship from industry professionals.

As a student-founded team in Ottawa, Team Plush is pioneering locally in Canada's
Capital, leaving a trail of future engineers for years to come.
