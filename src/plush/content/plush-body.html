03The season

From first meeting to the provincial stage.

Full schedule
  1. 1 we're here

    Sep–Dec · Offseason

    Offseason & preseason

    Before the game drops, we recruit new members, train on tools, CAD, and code, run fundraising and outreach, and prototype ideas so the team is ready to move on day one.

  2. 2 we're here

    January · Kickoff

    Kickoff & strategy

    The season opens with FIRST's global game reveal. We read the manual together, break down the field, and decide what kind of robot a rookie PLUSH team can build well.

  3. 3 we're here

    Weeks 1–2 · Design

    Design & prototype

    CAD in Onshape, cardboard-and-wood prototypes, and fast tests. We lock in a design that is simple enough to finish and sturdy enough to compete.

  4. 4 we're here

    Weeks 3–6 · Build

    Build & program

    Fabrication, wiring, and code come together on one robot. Drive practice starts the minute it can move, and every subsystem gets tested until it breaks.

  5. 5 we're here

    March · Districts

    District competitions

    Our first competitions: qualification matches, pit repairs between rounds, scouting alliance partners, and earning the ranking points that decide who advances.

  6. 6 we're here

    April · Provincials (if qualified)

    Ontario Provincial Championship

    The top district teams meet for the provincial title in front of a crowd of thousands, with a shot at qualifying for the FIRST Championship.

    AboutWhat's Team PLUSH?

    A second home for young engineers.

    Team PLUSH is a community FIRST Robotics Competition team, assembled from a group of passionate high school students building a functioning robot in under three months.

    hi, I'm Pal! I keep this team organized.

    The founding mission

    Ambitious missions always begin with a simple idea. For Team PLUSH, it began with three high school students who believed that STEM could be more than just innovation, but rather a secondary home where young engineers feel welcomed, supported, and inspired one day at a time.

    Our philosophy is to empower students to design, build, and test physical hardware without judgement, with mentorship from industry professionals. Team PLUSH will always operate on a student-led basis, with our oldest mentor being 18 years of age.

    As a student-founded team in Ottawa, Team PLUSH is pioneering locally in Canada's Capital, leaving a trail of future engineers for years to come. :)

    01The Rookie All-Star plan

    Our main goal, outside the robot itself.

    The Rookie All-Star Award is the biggest award a first-year FRC team can win. It goes to the rookie team that's already running like an established one, and the winner moves on to the FIRST Championship. Outside of the robot itself, this is our main goal for the season. Here's the plan.

    What judges look for, and what we're doing about it

    1. 01

      Support network

      Sponsor & mentor partnerships

      We're going for 20 sponsors across six tiers, with industry mentors working directly with each sub-team. Partners can host a private robot demo at their office.

    2. 02

      Spreading FIRST

      Community outreach

      At least 6 STEM workshops at local elementary and middle schools, plus 2 or more public robot demos with partners. Most of this happens before our first competition.

    3. 03

      Real-world impact

      Makers Making Change

      20 or more assistive-technology builds for people with disabilities. Each one gets logged and shared back to the Makers Making Change network.

    4. 04

      Access & culture

      Diversity, equity & inclusion

      We want a roster that actually looks like Ottawa, so we recruit underrepresented students into STEM and keep track of how representation changes each year.

    5. 05

      Long-term plan

      Sustainability plan

      A written plan for recruiting, funding, and mentoring so the team keeps going after the founders graduate. We're aiming for 45 students by 2027.

    6. 06

      On the field

      Robot & FIRST values

      A working competition robot built in under three months, and a team that treats other teams well in the pits.
