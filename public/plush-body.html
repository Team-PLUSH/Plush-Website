founders' mission

Ambitious missions always begin with a simple idea.

For Team PLUSH, it began with three high school students who believed that STEM could be more than just innovation, but rather a secondary home where young engineers feel welcomed, supported, and inspired one day at a time. Team PLUSH will ALWAYS operate on a student-led basis, with our oldest mentor being 18 years of age.

season plans

▲ start
  1. 1
    January · Kickoff

    Kickoff & Strategy

    The season opens with FIRST's global game reveal. We read the manual together, break down the field, and decide what kind of robot a rookie PLUSH team can build well.

  2. 2
    Weeks 1–2 · Design

    Design & Prototype

    CAD in Onshape, cardboard-and-wood prototypes, and fast tests. We lock in a design that is simple enough to finish and sturdy enough to compete.

  3. 3
    Weeks 3–6 · Build

    Build & Program

    Fabrication, wiring, and code come together on one robot. Drive practice starts the minute it can move, and every subsystem gets tested until it breaks.

  4. 4
    March + · Compete

    Competition

    Our first regional: qualification matches, pit repairs between rounds, scouting alliance partners, and cheering louder for other teams than for ourselves.

who we are

Team PLUSH is a student-run FIRST Robotics Competition team that believes robotics should feel welcoming, creative, and — yes — a little soft around the edges. We're engineers, artists, programmers, strategists, and humans who genuinely like each other.

We designed our brand around three pastel mascots on purpose. Walk into most FRC pits and you'll see industrial logos and intimidating robots. We wanted to show that technically excellent and openly friendly aren't opposites.

From CAD marathons to community demos to staying up way too late fixing autonomous routines the night before a regional — we do it together.

the rookie all-star plan

The Rookie All-Star Award is the highest honour a first-year FRC team can earn — it celebrates the rookie that already looks like a seasoned FIRST Impact Award contender, and the winner advances to the FIRST Championship. As FRC 11740's founding season, this is our number-one goal off the field. Here is exactly how we get there.

what the judges look for → what we're committing to

🤝
Sponsor & Mentor Partnerships
judged: strong support network
20 sponsors across six tiers, industry mentors embedded on every sub-team, and private robot demos hosted at partner offices.
📣
Community Outreach
judged: spreading FIRST
6+ elementary & middle-school STEM workshops and 2+ public robot demo events with partners — most of it before our first regional.
♿
Makers Making Change
judged: real-world impact
20+ assistive-technology builds for people with disabilities, logged and shared back to the Makers Making Change network.
🌍
Diversity, Equity & Inclusion
judged: access & culture
A roster that reflects Ottawa. We actively recruit underrepresented students into STEM and track representation season over season.
♻️
Sustainability Plan
judged: built to last
A documented recruitment, funding, and mentorship pipeline so Plush outlives its founders — targeting 45 students by the 2027 season.
🏆
Robot & FIRST Values
judged: the whole package
A competition-ready robot in under three months, Gracious Professionalism in the pits, and a team culture the judges can feel.

what we value

Every season we build a robot, but the most important machine we build is the way we work together. These values keep our team moving forward on and off the field.

team notebook
B
Build Boldly
Prototype quickly, test often, and treat every failed iteration as proof that we are learning faster than anyone else.
G
Gracious Competition
We compete hard, help teams harder, and believe a true win is when both alliances leave the field better than they arrived.
C
Curiosity First
We ask why as often as how. From software to strategy, every question is an invitation to learn and improve.
S
Soft Machine
Robots can be tough. Our team culture stays pastel, supportive, and centered around people before parts.
💡
Innovation
If it hasn't been done before, we draw it, CAD it, and build it with confidence.
🤝
Community
Our build space is open to everyone, our outreach is year-round, and we celebrate every local team.
📈
Progress
Small improvements matter. We track them, share them, and high-five them like the victory they are.
🎨
Creativity
We choose ideas that feel unexpected, useful, and a little bit soft — because the best robots are memorable.

privacy policy

Last updated: August 28, 2026

Team Plush ("PLUSH Robotics", "we", "us", "our") is a student-led, registered non-profit FIRST Robotics Competition team (FRC #11740) based in Ottawa, Ontario, Canada. We are committed to protecting the personal information of everyone who visits frc11740.ca (the "Site") or contacts our team.

The short version

This is a static, informational website. We do not use advertising cookies, analytics trackers, or third-party marketing tools, and we do not build profiles of visitors. We only receive personal information if you choose to send it to us.

Information we collect

  • Information you give us directly. If you email us or complete a form we link to (for example our "Join Us" form, hosted on Google Forms), we receive what you provide — typically your name, email address, school, and your message.
  • Newsletter sign-ups. If we offer an email newsletter and you subscribe, we collect your email address and a record of your consent so we can send you team updates. Every newsletter email includes an unsubscribe link.
  • Basic server logs. Our hosting provider automatically records standard technical data (such as IP address, browser type, and pages requested) to keep the Site secure and running. We do not use this data to identify individual visitors.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through this Site. Information about our student members and event participants (including minors) is collected offline through registration and media-release forms, with parent or guardian consent where required — not through this Site.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your questions and messages;
  • To process membership, volunteer, and sponsorship inquiries;
  • To send team updates and event invitations you have asked to receive;
  • To keep the Site secure and functioning.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

Service providers and storage outside Canada

We rely on a small number of trusted providers to operate:

  • Vercel Inc. hosts this Site. Its servers are located in the United States, so the technical data described above may be processed outside Canada and may be accessible to foreign authorities under the laws of that country.
  • Google LLC hosts the forms we link to and the email account we use (teamplushrobotics@gmail.com). Information you submit through those tools is also handled under Google's privacy policy.

We ask our providers to protect personal information in a manner consistent with this policy and with Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA).

Your choices and rights

You may ask us to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you, or withdraw your consent to receive emails, at any time. Contact us at teamplushrobotics@gmail.com and we will respond within a reasonable time. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada at priv.gc.ca.

Retention

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purpose it was provided, for our records as a non-profit organization, or as required by law, after which it is deleted or anonymized.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our website or activities change. The "last updated" date above will always reflect the current version.
